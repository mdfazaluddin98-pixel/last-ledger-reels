America Built Him a Monument… Then Erased His Name

America built a monument to one of its greatest Revolutionary War heroes…

and refused to put his name on it.
All it shows is a single marble boot.
The man inside that boot was Benedict Arnold — a fearless commander who helped win the Battle of Saratoga, one of the most important victories of the American Revolution. He was shot in the leg, crushed by his own horse, and still kept fighting.
But years of humiliation, stolen credit, and deep resentment turned him into something else entirely.
He secretly plotted to hand West Point — the fortress guarding the Hudson River — to the British in exchange for money and a high rank. The conspiracy almost succeeded… until everything collapsed.
This is the true story of how one of America’s greatest heroes became its most infamous traitor.
History is more than what we remember.

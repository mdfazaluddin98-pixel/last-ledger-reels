The Empire That Christianity Couldn’t Save

Did Christianity destroy the Roman Empire—or help preserve it?

Rome was once the most powerful empire in the ancient world. Then its western half collapsed. Some critics blamed Christianity for weakening traditional Roman values, military discipline, and loyalty to the state.

But there was another side.

The Eastern Roman Empire was deeply Christian—and survived for another thousand years. So if Christianity destroyed Rome, why did Constantinople last until 1453?

This short documentary explores the controversial debate, the rise of Christianity, and the many forces that brought down the Western Roman Empire.

The answer may be more complicated—and more surprising—than you think.

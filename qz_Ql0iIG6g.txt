They Mutilated the Emperor’s Nose — Then He Took Back His Throne

They cut off his nose in front of the entire city — a punishment designed to make sure he could never rule Rome again. It didn't work.

This is the true story of Justinian II, the only Byzantine Emperor in history to lose his throne, get mutilated and exiled to the edge of the known world... and come back to take it anyway.

Betrayed in Constantinople. Exiled to Crimea. Hunted by assassins he strangled with his bare hands. Saved by a foreign princess. Backed by an army of Bulgar horsemen. He swore an oath to God in the middle of a killing storm — and then crawled through an abandoned aqueduct in the dead of night to take back everything that was stolen from him.

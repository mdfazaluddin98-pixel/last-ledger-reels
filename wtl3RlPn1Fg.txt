5 Roman Punishments (The Last One Isn't a Crime)

Five ancient Roman punishments so brutal they sound made up. The last one was not even a crime — it was a job.

• Traitors were marched to the Tarpeian Rock and thrown off — and men were waiting at the bottom if the fall didn't finish the job.
• Vestal Virgins were buried alive rather than executed, because spilling their blood was considered a sin.
• Being the Roman Emperor was the most powerful job on earth, and one of the deadliest.

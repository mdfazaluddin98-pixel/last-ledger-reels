The Shortest War in History Lasted Just 38 Minutes 😳

At 9:00 AM, Zanzibar was still at peace. By 9:40, its palace was burning.

On August 27, 1896, the Anglo-Zanzibar War became the shortest war in recorded history. A ruler refused to surrender—and the British fleet opened fire.

The entire war lasted approximately 38 minutes. But its consequences lasted for decades.

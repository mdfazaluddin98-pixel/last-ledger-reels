The Roman Admiral Who Threw His Sacred Chickens Into the Sea

In 249 B.C., a Roman admiral faced a terrifying problem: his sacred chickens refused to eat. According to the ancient account, he responded by throwing them into the Mediterranean and declaring, “If they won’t eat, let them drink!”

Then he ordered the attack.

What followed was one of Rome’s most infamous naval disasters. Thousands of soldiers were captured or drowned, and the admiral returned home to face a punishment stranger than losing a battle.

Was Rome defeated by Carthage—or by its own arrogance?

Discover the unbelievable story of Rome’s sacred chickens, the Battle of Drepana, and the commander who challenged the gods.

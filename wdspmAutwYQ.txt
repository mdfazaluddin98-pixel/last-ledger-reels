He Boiled a Man Alive Just to Prove a Point

Some men chose to be crushed to death rather than speak. A rooster was tried for laying an egg. A man was boiled alive under a law written after his crime.

• In Basel, a rooster was burned for laying an egg because people feared it would hatch a basilisk.

Napoleon Escaped Prison—and Took France Back

Napoleon had lost his empire and been exiled to a tiny island. France had a new king, and the army was ordered to stop him.

Then, on March 1, 1815, Napoleon landed in France with just over 600 men.

What happened next was unbelievable. Soldiers sent to arrest him abandoned their orders and joined his army. The king fled. Paris fell back into Napoleon’s hands.

But Europe was waiting. Napoleon had only one chance to survive—and it ended at Waterloo.

Follow the astonishing story of Napoleon’s return from exile, the army that changed sides, and the 100 days that terrified Europe.

One man. One comeback. One final battle.

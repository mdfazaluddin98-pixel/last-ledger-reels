Hannibal: The Man Who Almost Destroyed Rome

Rome had never faced an enemy like Hannibal.

In 218 BC, Hannibal Barca led an army out of Carthage, crossed the Alps with war elephants, and invaded Italy. What followed shocked the ancient world.

He destroyed Roman armies at Trebia and Lake Trasimene. Then, at Cannae, he delivered one of the worst defeats in Roman history.

Tens of thousands of Roman soldiers were killed.

For a moment, Rome itself seemed to be in danger.

But Hannibal faced a question that still fascinates historians:

After defeating Rome’s armies, why didn’t he capture Rome?

This documentary follows Hannibal’s incredible campaign—from the crossing of the Alps to the battlefield victories that nearly brought the Roman Republic to its knees—and the decision that may have changed the course of history.

He defeated Rome’s armies.
He terrified Rome’s allies.
He came closer to destroying Rome than almost anyone before him.

But he never captured the city.

The Last Ledger explores the people, places, ideas, and moments that shaped human history.

How Vikings Survived −30°C Winters Without Electricity

How did Vikings survive freezing Scandinavian winters without electricity, central heating, or modern insulation?

Their secret was not one incredible invention. It was an entire survival system built around longhouses, central hearths, thick walls, wool clothing, animal skins, food storage, and careful preparation.

But there was one thing that could turn a warm Viking home into a death trap.

This short documentary explores how Viking families fought the cold—and why keeping the fire burning was only part of the solution.

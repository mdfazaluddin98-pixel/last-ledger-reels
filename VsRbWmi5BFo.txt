The Pope Who Turned the Vatican Into a Family Empire

In Renaissance Rome, one pope became the center of a scandal that sounded more like a royal dynasty than the history of the Church. Pope Alexander VI, born Rodrigo Borgia, was accused of using his power, wealth, and family connections to control Rome while openly maintaining relationships and fathering children. His family became one of the most powerful—and controversial—forces in Renaissance Italy. But how much of the legend was true, and how much was exaggerated by his enemies? In this episode of The Last Ledger, we uncover the rise of the Borgia family, the shocking reputation of Alexander VI, and the political world that made his reign possible. History is more than what we remember.

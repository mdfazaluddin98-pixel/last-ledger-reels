30,000 Men Went to War Over a Bucket (And Still Lost)

In 1325, Bologna sent thirty thousand men to war over a stolen wooden bucket. They outnumbered Modena five to one. They still lost.

• The theft: a few Modenese soldiers slipped into Bologna and took a bucket from a well.
• The battle: at Zappolino, Modena's cavalry broke Bologna's horsemen and the foot soldiers scattered.
• The prize: Bologna never got the bucket back. It's still in Modena today.

Roman Soldiers Put Nails in Their Boots on Purpose

Roman soldiers' caligae had dozens of iron hobnails in every sole for grip and wear on long marches. In 70 AD, at the Temple in Jerusalem, those nails made the centurion Julian slip on the stone pavement, and he was killed. The historian Josephus called him the finest soldier he saw in the war.

The Real Story of 300 (Why He Chose Only Fathers)

In 480 BC, a Spartan king chose three hundred men for a mission none of them would survive, and every one of them had to already have a living son.

• The pass: to reach Greece, the Persian army had to squeeze through a narrow gap between cliffs and sea, the Hot Gates.
• The stand: for three days, wave after wave broke against the Spartan shield wall.
• The betrayal: a local Greek showed the Persians a secret path, and Leonidas sent the rest of the army away and stayed.

In 1518, Hundreds of People Started Dancing. Nobody Knows Why.

In 1518, a woman in Strasbourg began dancing in the street. Within weeks, hundreds of people were reportedly caught in the same strange phenomenon. Some accounts describe exhaustion, collapse, and deaths. And the authorities made a decision that sounds unbelievable today: they encouraged more dancing.

What caused the Dancing Plague of 1518? Nobody knows for certain.

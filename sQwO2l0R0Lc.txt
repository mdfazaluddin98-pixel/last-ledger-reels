The Ottoman Army Saw This and Turned Back

In 1462, the most powerful army on Earth marched to conquer a tiny European kingdom. The conquest should have taken weeks.

Instead, the empire that had just conquered Constantinople turned around and retreated — not because it lost a battle, but because of what it found waiting on the road to the capital.

This is the story of Vlad the Third, the ruler history remembers by another name: Dracula.

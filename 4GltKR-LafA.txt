The 250-Year-Old Myth About the Fall of an Empire

In 476 AD, the Western Roman Empire ended without a final battle. No burning capital. Just a teenage emperor, quietly removed from power.

For centuries, one historian's theory blamed a shocking suspect for the collapse. But there's a problem with his case that most people never hear about.

This is the story of Edward Gibbon's theory, the empire that should have disproven it, and what actually killed Rome.

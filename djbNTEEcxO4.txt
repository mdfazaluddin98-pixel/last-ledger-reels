Henry VIII Broke England Away From Rome

King Henry VIII wanted the pope to approve his divorce. When Rome refused, he made a decision that would transform England forever.

In 1534, Henry became the supreme head of the Church of England. He broke with Rome, seized monastery wealth, and changed the religious and political future of the country.

But the most shocking part came later. The marriage he risked everything to obtain ended in tragedy—and the daughter he left behind would become one of England’s greatest monarchs.

One king. One divorce. One decision that changed England forever.

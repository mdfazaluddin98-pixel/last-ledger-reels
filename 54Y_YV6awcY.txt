They Killed Julius Caesar. Then They Started Dying.

More than sixty men joined the conspiracy to kill Julius Caesar.

They believed they were saving the Roman Republic.

But in the years that followed, the conspirators were hunted, captured, killed in battle, or took their own lives.

From Trebonius and Decimus Brutus to Cassius and Brutus, this is the story of what happened to the men behind one of history's most famous assassinations.

They killed one man to stop Rome from becoming an empire.

Instead, they helped create one.

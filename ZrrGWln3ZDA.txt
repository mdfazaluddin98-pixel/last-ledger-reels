He Built an Army for the Afterlife—Then Poisoned Himself

Qin Shi Huang conquered China, unified its rival kingdoms, and became the first emperor of a nation that would last for centuries. But the man who could command an empire could not command death. Beneath the earth, he built a vast terracotta army to guard his tomb. Ancient accounts also describe a brutal campaign against scholars who challenged his authority—and a desperate search for immortality that may have involved poisonous mercury. In this episode of The Last Ledger, discover how China’s first emperor tried to control both the living world and the afterlife, and how his obsession with eternal power helped create one of history’s greatest mysteries. The emperor wanted to live forever. His army survived him instead.

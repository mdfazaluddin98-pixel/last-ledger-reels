They Pressed the Detonator... But This Invincible Bridge Refused to Die

In March 1945, German engineers pressed a button to destroy the last bridge across the Rhine. It refused to fall.

• The trap: the bridge was wired with heavy explosives, and all they had to do was turn a key.
• The failure: nothing happened. A backup charge lifted the bridge into the air, and it settled back onto its foundations.
• The result: American soldiers cut the wires, and thousands of troops crossed the river by nightfall.

Rome Drilled Holes in Its Bullets on Purpose

At Burnswark Hill in Scotland, archaeologists found Roman lead sling bullets from the 2nd century AD. Some had a hole drilled through them so they whistled in flight. Other Roman bullets, found in Italy, were cast with messages for the enemy, like FERI POMP, "hit Pompey".

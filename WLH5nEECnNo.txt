The Roman Emperor Who Was Baptized on His Deathbed

He changed the Roman Empire forever.

In 313 AD, Constantine helped establish religious toleration for Christianity. A religion once persecuted by Rome was now protected by its emperor.

He supported the Church, built Christian churches, and helped move Christianity toward the center of imperial power.

But there was one surprising detail:

Constantine was not baptized until he was seriously ill and near death.

In 337 AD, near Nicomedia, the emperor finally received baptism. Why did he wait so long? Some Christians delayed baptism until late in life, but we cannot know Constantine’s exact reason.

The man who transformed Christianity before his baptism died shortly afterward.

History is rarely as simple as the stories we remember.

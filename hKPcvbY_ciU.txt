Roman Emperor vs. Christian Bishop: The Day Rome’s Power Was Challenged

In AD 390, a Roman emperor ordered a massacre. Then a Christian bishop refused to let him enter a church.

Emperor Theodosius I was one of the most powerful men in the ancient world. But after the massacre of Thessalonica, he faced an extraordinary challenge from Ambrose, the bishop of Milan.

The emperor had armies.
The bishop had no army.
Yet the bishop stood at the church door and demanded that the emperor repent.

Could a Christian bishop really challenge the ruler of Rome?

And did this confrontation change the relationship between Christianity, morality, and political power in the Western world?

This documentary explores the massacre of Thessalonica, the confrontation between Theodosius and Ambrose, and the moment when the power of Rome met the authority of the Church.

An emperor. A massacre. A bishop. A confrontation that changed history.

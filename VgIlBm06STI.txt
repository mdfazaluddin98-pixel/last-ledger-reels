Atomic Bomb: The Day America Changed History

On July 25, 1945, President Harry Truman approved an order that would change the world forever. The atomic bomb test had succeeded, Japan had not surrendered, and American leaders were weighing the terrible cost of continuing the war against the consequences of using a weapon unlike anything humanity had ever seen. What happened inside the Allied headquarters that day? What choices were considered—and what did Truman actually authorize? In this episode of The Last Ledger, we follow the decision that led to Hiroshima and Nagasaki, the arguments surrounding it, and the beginning of the nuclear age. The war ended, but the world would never be the same.

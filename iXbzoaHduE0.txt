Caesar Hid Spikes in the Ground on Purpose

At the siege of Alesia in 52 BC, Caesar's soldiers dug rows of pits with a sharpened, fire-hardened stake in each one and hid them under brush. Caesar wrote that the men called them "lilies" because of how they looked. The Gallic relief army failed, and Vercingetorix surrendered.

The Army That Weaponized Cats    #history #facts #ancient #ancienthistory #story

An ancient superpower was completely destroyed by an invading army holding live cats. Or at least, that is the lie history chose to remember.

• The legend: Persia's army carried live cats to the front, and Egypt's archers wouldn't shoot.
• The catch: the only writer who tells it lived about seven hundred years after the battle.
• The twist: a Greek general named Phanes switched sides and helped Persia.

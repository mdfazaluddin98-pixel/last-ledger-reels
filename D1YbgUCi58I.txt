Rome Executed Jesus. Then Christianity Changed Rome.

Rome executed Jesus under the authority of Pontius Pilate. But less than three hundred years later, Roman emperors would embrace Christianity—and the religion once feared by the empire would become central to its power.

How did a movement born under Roman execution become one of the most influential forces in Roman history?

In this short documentary, we explore the connection between Jesus and the Roman Empire, the political danger of his message, his crucifixion, the rise of Christianity, and Constantine’s dramatic transformation of Rome.

The empire that once condemned Jesus would eventually build churches in his name.

History is more than what we remember. This is The Last Ledger.

The Emperor Who Blinded an Entire Army

In 1014, Byzantine Emperor Basil II defeated the Bulgarian army at the Battle of Kleidion.

But according to later medieval accounts, the punishment that followed was more horrifying than the battle itself.

Thousands of captured soldiers were reportedly blinded. One man in every hundred was supposedly left with a single eye so he could lead the others home.

Imagine surviving a battlefield…

Only to be sent back to your king as a warning.

When the Bulgarian ruler Samuel reportedly saw the survivors, he collapsed and died shortly afterward.

The exact number of prisoners is uncertain, but the story became one of the most disturbing episodes in Byzantine history.

Basil would later be remembered as “the Bulgar-Slayer.”

The battle ended in 1014. But the fear created by that victory lasted much longer.

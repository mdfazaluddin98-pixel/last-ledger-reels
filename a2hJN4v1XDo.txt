The Man Who Nearly Started Nuclear War

On October 27, 1962, the world was trapped in the Cuban Missile Crisis. But deep beneath the sea, a Soviet submarine was surrounded by American forces, running out of air, and carrying a nuclear torpedo.

The crew did not know whether war had already begun.

One officer, Vasili Arkhipov, played a crucial role in preventing the launch. But he did not act alone—and the exact conversation inside the submarine remains debated.

This short documentary explores the terrifying decision beneath the sea and the moment the world came dangerously close to nuclear war.

Sometimes history changes because one person refuses to make the worst decision possible.

History is more than what we remember. This is The Last Ledger.

Varus Lost 3 Legions. Then He Lost His Head

After the Teutoburg disaster, the Roman general Varus's head was passed from a German chief to a king, and on to the emperor.

Sources: Velleius Paterculus 2.119; Cassius Dio, Roman History 56.21-22

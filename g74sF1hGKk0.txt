200 British Ships Faced This Broken Admiral (Then Everything Went Wrong)

In 1739, Britain went to war over a man's ear. Two years later, a one-eyed, one-legged, one-armed Spanish admiral beat two hundred British ships.

• The insult: in 1731, Spanish coast guards boarded a British ship, and the story goes that they cut off Captain Robert Jenkins' ear.
• The war: seven years later he showed it to Parliament, and Britain declared war.
• The disaster: at Cartagena in 1741, Admiral Blas de Lezo, nicknamed Half-Man, held the city against a fleet of almost two hundred ships.

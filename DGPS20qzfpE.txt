Hitler Was Fooled By a Stolen Corpse

In nineteen forty three, the Allied forces prepared to invade Europe. They faced an impenetrable wall of German armor.

They desperately needed a spy to feed false information to Adolf Hitler. So British intelligence recruited the perfect agent. There was only one problem. He was already dead. The target was the island of Sicily. Hitler knew the invasion was coming. He moved massive armies to defend the beaches. The British needed to convince the German High Command that the real target was Greece. They could not risk a living spy breaking under torture. So they found the body of a dead man in London. They dressed the corpse in the uniform of a Royal Marine. They named him Major William Martin. They gave him a fabricated life. They put a photo of a fake fiancée in his wallet. They put used theater tickets in his pockets. Then they chained a briefcase to his wrist. Inside the briefcase were forged top secret documents detailing a massive Allied invasion of Greece. In the dead of night, a British submarine surfaced off the coast of Spain. The crew pushed the body into the freezing ocean. They let the tide carry him to the shore. Spanish authorities found the corpse. They immediately passed the contents of the briefcase to Nazi intelligence officers. The documents made their way directly to Berlin. German analysts scrutinized every piece of paper. They analyzed the water damage. They debated the theater tickets. If they spotted a single flaw in the forgery, the Allied invasion of Sicily would become a total slaughter. British commanders sat in London and waited in absolute silence. Then they intercepted a German transmission. Adolf Hitler took the bait completely. He ordered entire Panzer divisions to abandon Sicily and deploy to Greece. When the Allied forces finally stormed the Sicilian beaches, the heavy German resistance was gone. The invasion was a massive success. The Allies shifted the momentum of the Second World War. They outsmarted the intelligence network of the Third Reich. And they did it because Adolf Hitler put his absolute faith in a man who never existed. Britain's greatest spy was a stolen corpse.

Cleopatra’s Final Days Were Far More Horrific Than History Admits

In 30 BCE, Cleopatra was trapped in Alexandria as Octavian’s forces closed in. Her kingdom was collapsing, Mark Antony was dead, and Rome wanted to turn the last queen of Egypt into a symbol of its victory.

But Cleopatra faced a final choice: submit to Rome and be paraded through its streets—or find another way to control how her story ended.

Ancient sources disagree on some details of her death, but the final days of Cleopatra reveal a brutal struggle over power, dignity, and control.

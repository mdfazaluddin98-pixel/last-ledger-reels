France Went to War Over a Pastry Bill (Then Mexico Made One Huge Mistake)

In 1838, France invaded Mexico. It started with a pastry shop.

• The claim: a French chef said Mexican officers had looted his bakery, and demanded sixty thousand pesos for it.
• The war: France backed the claim, added it to a list of old debts, and blockaded Mexico's ports when it refused to pay six hundred thousand.
• The twist: French cannons wounded a disgraced Mexican general so badly his leg was amputated on the spot, and the injury made him a hero.

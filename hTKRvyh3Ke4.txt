Genghis Khan Turned a City Into a Polo Field

In 1221, the Mongols leveled the city of Nishapur so flat that their riders played polo where it had stood. It started with one arrow: an archer on the walls killed a Mongol general who turned out to be Genghis Khan's son-in-law.

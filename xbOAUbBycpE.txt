The Knights Templar Had Everything—Until the King Came for Their Wealth

On Friday the 13th, October 1307, the Knights Templar were arrested across France. This powerful military order had wealth, land, soldiers, and direct protection from the pope. So why did the king suddenly turn against them?

Behind the arrests was Philip IV of France, a king desperate for money and determined to eliminate a powerful rival. But destroying the Templars required the cooperation of Pope Clement V—and the accusations of heresy, secret rituals, and corruption would change history forever.

Discover how the Knights Templar were betrayed, tortured, suppressed, and transformed into one of history’s greatest mysteries.

History is more than what we remember. The Last Ledger explores the people, places, ideas, events, and stories that shaped our world.
